Global Coastal Evolution Modelling

Developing a unified planetary view of coastal evolution by coupling long-term shoreline models with global climate and sea-level rise data. This project aims to simulate how coastlines will change over the next century.

Forecasting Hurricane Impacts

Operational coastal erosion and flooding models for hurricane impact forecasting. This project integrates real-time weather data with morphodynamic models to predict storm surge and beach erosion along vulnerable coastlines.

Estuarine Sediment Dynamics

Investigating the morphodynamic response of estuarine intertidal shoals to sea-level rise and changing sediment supplies. This research helps inform nature-based solutions and estuary management.

Nature-Based Coastal Protection

Evaluating the effectiveness of mangroves, dunes, and coral reefs in reducing coastal erosion and flooding. The project uses physics-based models to quantify the protective services provided by natural ecosystems.
